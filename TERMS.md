## 4. Data about real people

**Read this section before you make an identity.**

An identity holds a description of a person and a transcript of the messages
of that person.
That is personal data, and in many places the law protects it.
The messages in a transcript also belong in part to each other person in the
conversation.

You are responsible for the data that you put in an identity.

- Get the consent of the person before you make an identity of that person.
- Keep the identity on your own computer. The Software keeps it there, and
  `.gitignore` keeps it out of the repository.
- Delete the identity when the person asks you to.

## 5. What the Software writes is not the person

## 6. Language models

the terms of its operator then apply.

The Software does not supply a model.
